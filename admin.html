Cleaning Business Risk Register & Safeguards
Key operational, legal, and financial risks in residential & commercial cleaning — and how your site policies protect Bovic Premier.
View Terms & Risks Page View Privacy Page
1. Property Damage & Fragile Items
Breakage of art, collectibles, TVs, or brittle blinds.
Safeguard: Terms exclude unstable items, require securing valuables, set 48-hr claim window, and cap liability (covered via general liability insurance).
2. Surface & Chemical Etching
Acidic/abrasive cleaners damaging natural marble, travertine, brass, or waxed hardwood.
Safeguard: Pre-existing wear documented via before/after photos; clients disclose specialty finishes before service.
3. Biohazard, Mold & Height Injuries
Worker exposure to blood/bodily fluids, pet waste, mold, or ladder falls.
Safeguard: Policy explicitly bars biohazards, mold remediation, 30+ lb lifting, and ladders above 2-step stools.
4. Key, Alarm & Gate Access Liability
Lost keys, false alarm fines, or unauthorized disclosure of entry codes.
Safeguard: Entry notes hidden behind authenticated worker login; Privacy Policy limits access strictly to assigned cleaner.
5. Pet Safety & Bites / Escapes
Anxious pets reacting to vacuums or slipping out open doors.
Safeguard: Mandatory booking disclosure of pets; Terms require crating/securing anxious animals during service.
6. Late Cancellations, Lockouts & Underquoted Homes
Lost crew hours from last-minute cancels, locked doors, or heavily soiled homes booked at base price.
Safeguard: 24-hr cancellation / $50 lockout policy + pre-work walkthrough adjustment clause in Terms.
7. Chargebacks & Unpaid Invoices
Disputed card charges or unpaid Pay-Later bookings.
Safeguard: Policy terms agreed at checkout + timestamped checklists & before/after photo verification for disputes.
8. Worker Classification, Bonding & Insurance
Misclassification fines or uninsured cleaner accidents (Georgia W-2 vs 1099 & Workers' Comp rules).
Safeguard: Maintain active General Liability + Janitorial Bond + Workers' Comp as team scales, and clear staff onboarding.
