10 Tips to Keep Your Home Sparkling & Fresh

A clean home is easier to keep than you think. A few small habits each day stop mess from piling up, so your weekend is yours again.

  • Make beds every morning. It takes two minutes and the whole room looks tidier.

  • Wipe kitchen counters and the stovetop after every meal.

  • Squeegee the shower glass after use to stop soap scum and water spots.

  • Keep a basket in each room for things that belong elsewhere.

  • Take out trash and recycling before bins overflow.

  • Dust from top to bottom: shelves and fans first, floors last.

  • Use a doormat inside and outside each entry to keep dirt off your floors.

  • Run the dishwasher every night and empty it in the morning.

  • Wash towels and bed linens weekly.

  • Book a deep clean a few times a year for the spots daily cleaning misses.

Because Every Space Deserves a Fresh Start

Moving in, moving out, or just feeling like your home needs a reset? A deep clean gets into the places regular cleaning skips: baseboards, inside the oven and fridge, the range hood, tile grout and ceiling fans. It is the best way to start a new routine, and we recommend it for a first visit.

Your Guide to a Healthier, Cleaner Home

Dust, pet hair and bathroom moisture affect the air you breathe. Vacuum carpets and rugs at least weekly, wipe high-touch spots like door handles, light switches and remotes, and keep bathrooms dry and ventilated to prevent mildew. Choose gentle, eco-friendly products, especially if you have kids, pets or allergies.

Best Ways to Keep Your House Fresh & Clean

Open windows for a few minutes each day, clean the fridge out weekly, and wash bins and drains every couple of weeks. Fresh air and a clean kitchen remove most household odors without heavy fragrances.

Floor Cleaning & Care

Vacuum before you mop so you are not pushing grit around. Use a damp (not wet) mop on hardwood with a cleaner made for wood, and give tile grout a scrub every few months to keep it bright.

Great Tips to Choose a Professional Cleaner

Look for clear, upfront pricing, a checklist of what is included, and a re-clean promise if something is missed. Ask whether they bring their own supplies and whether you need to be home. At Bovic Premier we confirm your exact price before cleaning day, send a before/after photo report, and re-clean anything you're not happy with within 48 hours.
