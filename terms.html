Terms, Policies & Risks

  • Home
  • Terms, Policies & Risks

1. Booking, Walkthroughs & Pricing Policy

All published prices are starting rates for homes in typical condition. If a home requires substantially more time due to heavy buildup, undeclared square footage, post-construction dust, or hoarding conditions, our team will confirm an adjusted estimate with you before beginning work.

2. 24-Hour Cancellation, Rescheduling & Lockout Policy

You may reschedule or cancel free of charge at least 24 hours before your arrival window. Cancellations under 24 hours or lockouts where cleaners wait 15+ minutes without entry access (missing gate codes, unreturned calls, or locked doors) may incur a $50 late-cancellation / lockout fee.

3. 48-Hour Satisfaction Guarantee

Your satisfaction is guaranteed. If any area on your room-by-room checklist was missed, notify us within 48 hours of service with a quick note or photo and we will return promptly to re-clean the missed spot at no extra charge. Refunds are not issued once a re-clean option is declined.

4. Booking, Payment & Chargeback Terms

Bookings scheduled online through our Cal.com calendar are confirmed by email, and we confirm your exact price before cleaning day. Invoices unpaid 48 hours after completion are subject to late fees and suspension of recurring discounts. Unwarranted payment chargebacks after completed service will be contested with signed/timestamped checklists and verification photos.

5. Health, Safety & Service Exclusions

For team safety and regulatory compliance, cleaners do NOT handle biohazards (blood, bodily fluids, human/pet waste, litter boxes), mold/asbestos remediation, pest infestations, exterior upper-story windows, moving furniture over 30 lbs, climbing ladders higher than a 2-step stool, or dishwashing/heavy clutter organizing unless explicitly agreed.

6. Cleaning Business Risks & Liability Disclosures

Cleaning Risk & Liability Disclosure: (1) Pre-existing Wear & Fragile Surfaces — we are not liable for pre-existing scratches, unsealed marble/granite etching, loose grout/tiles, brittle blinds, unstable shelves, or worn paint/wallpaper. (2) Valuables & Electronics — please secure cash, jewelry, heirlooms, fragile collectibles, firearms, and delicate electronics before arrival. (3) Pets & Home Access — pets anxious around vacuums or new people must be crated or secured; please share accurate alarm/gate codes. (4) Surface & Chemical Compatibility — notify us of specialty finishes or custom cleaners you prefer us to use. (5) Damage Claims — any accidental breakage must be reported within 48 hours; maximum liability is limited to repair/replacement value up to $1,000 or 2x the cleaning fee (whichever is greater) unless covered by general liability insurance.
